Bingo Agent Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Bingo Agent is an internal bookkeeping tool used only by the owner of Placentia Platinum Bingo. It is not available to the public.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, it accesses the owner's own Google Sheets workbook, the business's Session Report Google Form responses, and photos those responses upload to the owner's Google Drive.

How the data is used

Only to calculate and record the business's session results, inventory, expenses and monthly reports in the owner's own workbook.

Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
