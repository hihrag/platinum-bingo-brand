Bingo Agent

Bingo Agent is a private, internal tool used by Placentia Platinum Bingo (operating for Youth Help Center, 901 N. Bradford Ave., Placentia, CA) to keep its own books.

It reads the business's own Google Form submissions, Google Sheets workbook and Google Drive photos to record session results, inventory and monthly reports. It is not offered to the public and is used only by the business owner.
